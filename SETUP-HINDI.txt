  3. "Add file" button dabao > "Upload files" chuno.
  4. Extract kiye hue folder ki SAARI cheezen drag karke browser me chhodo:
     README.md, banner.gif, aur .github folder.
     (Purani files replace ho jayengi - puche to confirm kar dena.)
  5. Neeche green "Commit changes" button dabao.
  6. Upar "Actions" tab kholo. Pehli baar ho to enable karne wala
     button dabao.
  7. Left side me "Generate snake animation" pe click karo >
     "Run workflow" > green button dabao.
  8. "GitHub-Profile-3D-Contrib" pe click karo >
     "Run workflow" > green button dabao.
  9. 2-4 minute ruko. Phir kholo: github.com/Rizwanshaikh1410
     Tumhari animated 3D profile live hogi!
